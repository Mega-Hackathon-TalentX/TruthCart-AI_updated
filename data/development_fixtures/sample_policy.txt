=== SECTION: RETURNS ===
Title: Return & Exchange Policy
Policy ID: POLICY:RETURNS
Content:
Customers may return eligible items within 14 calendar days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. To be eligible for return, items must be unworn, unwashed, with all original tags attached and in original packaging. Clearance items marked 'Final Sale' and personal hygiene items are non-returnable. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse.

=== SECTION: SHIPPING ===
Title: Shipping & Delivery Policy
Policy ID: POLICY:SHIPPING
Content:
We offer Standard Shipping (3-5 business days) at a flat fee of ₹99, and Express Shipping (1-2 business days) at ₹199. Standard shipping is completely free for all orders with a cart subtotal above ₹1,499. Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched on the same day. Tracking information is sent via SMS and email immediately upon shipment. We currently deliver to all serviceable PIN codes across India.

=== SECTION: WARRANTY ===
Title: Product Warranty Policy
Policy ID: POLICY:WARRANTY
Content:
All consumer electronic products purchased through TruthCart include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of invoice. The warranty covers internal hardware malfunctions and defective components under normal usage. It strictly does not cover accidental liquid damage, physical drops, unauthorised modifications, or normal cosmetic wear and tear. For warranty claims, customers must present the original tax invoice.
